1. OPTICAL & BASE IMAGERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      Mars 2020 Jezero Crater Landing Site Products (HiRISE & CTX Mosaics)
Instrument:   High Resolution Imaging Science Experiment (HiRISE) & Context Camera (CTX)
Missions:     Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) / Mars 2020 Perseverance
Provider:     USGS Astrogeology Science Center / NASA Planetary Data System (PDS)
URL:          https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars-2020-jezero-crater-landing-site-products
Usage in App: Primary optical base layer showing Jezero Crater Western Delta fan,
              crater rim structures, and floor terrain for hyper-local astronaut navigation.

2. TOPOGRAPHY & DIGITAL ELEVATION MODEL (DEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      Mars Sample Return Terrain-Relative Navigation HiRISE DTM Mosaic & MOLA/HRSC
Instrument:   HiRISE DTM (Digital Terrain Model) and Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) /
              High Resolution Stereo Camera (HRSC)
Missions:     MRO / Mars Global Surveyor / Mars Express
Provider:     USGS Astrogeology / NASA PDS Geosciences Node
URL:          https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars-sample-return-terrain-relative-navigation-hirise-dtm-mosaic
              https://www.usgs.gov/data/mars-2020-terrain-relative-navigation-ctx-dtm-mosaic
Usage in App: High-resolution surface elevation contours, slope computation, steep hazard
              identification, and real-time route elevation cross-section profiling.

3. MINERALOGY & SPECTRAL COMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      CRISM Spectral Parameter Products & Mars Trek Mineral Spectroscopy Overlays
Instrument:   Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars (CRISM)
Mission:      Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO)
Provider:     NASA PDS Geosciences Node / NASA Solar System Treks (Mars Trek)
URL:          https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/
Usage in App: Mineral deposit layers identifying Olivine-Carbonate units (Target A),
              Clay / Phyllosilicate minerals (Target B), and Deltaic Sandstones (Target C)
              crucial for biosignature preservation and paleolake water history.

4. SURFACE WEATHER & ATMOSPHERIC TELEMETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer (MEDA) Atmospheric Data
Instrument:   MEDA Suite (Ambient Temperature Sensors, Pressure Sensor, Wind Sensors, Dust Optical Depth)
Mission:      Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover (Jezero Crater floor)
Provider:     Centro de Astrobiologia (CAB) / NASA PDS Atmospheres Node
URL:          https://atmos.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/logs/MARS2020_MEDA_ANC_Derived_Del7.htm
Usage in App: Real-time mission environment panel and HUD indicators:
              - Ambient Temperature: -12 °C (mid-day seasonal peak)
              - Horizontal Wind Speed: 6 m/s
              - Atmospheric Opacity / Dust Level: Low (Tau nominal)
              - Atmospheric Pressure: ~610 Pa

5. IONIZING RADIATION FIGURES (SURFACE GCR ANALOG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      Radiation Assessment Detector (RAD) Surface Dose Measurements
Instrument:   RAD (MSL Science Payload)
Mission:      Mars Science Laboratory (Curiosity Rover, Gale Crater analog)
Provider:     Southwest Research Institute (SwRI) / NASA PDS
URL:          https://data.nasa.gov/
Note:         Perseverance does not carry an onboard radiation detector. Curiosity RAD
              measurements and published NASA Space Radiation Laboratory studies provide
              the verified surface Galactic Cosmic Ray (GCR) baseline.
Usage in App: Astronaut EVA dosimeter and HUD readout:
              - Baseline surface dose rate: ~0.60 µSv/h (~210 µGy/day)
              - Integrated safety score constraint for Go / No-Go decision matrix.

6. GROUND-LEVEL POV IMAGERY & ROVER TARGET SCIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset:      Mars 2020 Perseverance Raw Images API / Science Target Dossiers
Instrument:   Mastcam-Z, SuperCam Remote Micro-Imager (RMI), Navigation Cameras (Navcam)
Mission:      Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover
Provider:     NASA / JPL-Caltech / MSSS
URL:          https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/multimedia/raw-images/
Usage in App: Target card rover POV photography, scientific value scoring (Water history,
              Biosignatures, Geology), and sample collection field logs for Jezero exploration.
